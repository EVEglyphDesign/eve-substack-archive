News Updates!

  • Los Gatos, California, September 2025

Since I launched Frictionless Data a year ago, the number of people reading this newsletter has grown eight times larger. These CIOs, CFOs, data engineers, sales leaders, and analysts want to use data for better, faster decisions.

With this in mind, let me reintroduce Frictionless Data to you.

A reader recently described my newsletter as "how to manage data, written for leaders." I love that, because it perfectly describes my commitment to you: providing trusted advice and counterintuitive ideas, with clear, concise writing that anyone can understand.

Through a year of publishing weekly newsletters, I discovered this: you write to learn. Writing isn't a data dump of wisdom accumulated from past experiences. Instead, you start to see connections that were there all the time, waiting to surface. Your feedback, questions, and stories help push these ideas forward.
